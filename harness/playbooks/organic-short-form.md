# Playbook: short-form video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

Per platform:
- **Cadence:** posts/week and days since the last post. Five or more a week is a machine (team or creator network); under one is a habit, not a channel.
- **Reach vs followers:** median views against follower count. Median far above followers means the algorithm distributes them (the hooks work); top video far above median means hits with a long tail.
- **Hooks:** quote the top 5 by views and classify: POV skit, "I tried…", problem → solution, green-screen reaction, listicle, tutorial, founder talking head, trend/sound. The wording is the asset; do not paraphrase it into marketing language.
- **Length and cut rhythm:** if every top video is under 20 seconds with a cut every 2 seconds, the format is the message.
- **Face vs faceless:** character-driven hooks point to UGC creators or a creator network; screen-recording hooks point to a product-led loop.
- **Organic → paid:** the same hook appearing in ads three weeks later is the loop. Say it when you see it.
- **Where the CTA lands:** bio link, pinned comment, app store, landing page. One destination per account.

Copyable output: cadence target, the 3 hook templates to adapt (structure, not copy), the length and cut rhythm, and the one destination a viewer is sent to.
