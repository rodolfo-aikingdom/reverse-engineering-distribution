# Playbook: affiliate and referral

Affiliate turns other people's audiences into distribution, and it only works when the product is easy to explain in one line and the payout is legible.

What to extract:
- **Program existence and terms:** commission percentage, recurring or one-time, cookie window, minimum payout. Quote what the page states.
- **The tracking stack:** Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tapfiliate, or a homegrown /ref/ parameter on links.
- **Who promotes them:** affiliates you can find in the wild (review sites, newsletters, comparison pages, YouTube). Their framing is the offer in someone else's words.
- **What the affiliate gets:** a swipe file, a demo account, screenshots. Programs that supply assets get used; programs that only pay do not.
- **Referral loop for users:** in-product referral with a two-sided reward, and where it sits in the lifecycle.

Copyable output: the commission to match, the three assets to hand every affiliate (one-liner, 3 hooks, one screenshot), and the referral placement in the product.
