# Playbook: paid as amplification, not as a channel

Paid is the last step, not the first: it puts budget behind a hook that already worked. A plan that starts with paid is a plan to buy data at the highest possible price.

What to extract:
- **Volume and longevity:** active ads now, ads in the library, launches in the last 30 days, and the run length of the longest-running active ads. Sixty days is a winner; thirty days of variants is testing.
- **Hook reuse:** whether the ad copy matches an organic post. If it does, the loop is organic-first and the paid budget is downstream of content.
- **Formats:** video UGC, static, carousel, demo screen recording. Which one repeats across the winners.
- **Landing pages:** the same page for every ad, or a page per angle. UTM campaign names reveal their internal naming and the structure of their tests.
- **Who else bids:** resellers, affiliates, agencies advertising on the brand. It says the category has margin.
- **Pixel stack:** Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, Apple Search Ads attribution. A pixel is intent, not spend.

Copyable output: the three winners to adapt, the format and length to shoot, the landing page to build per angle, and the gate — no paid until one organic post beats the median three times.
