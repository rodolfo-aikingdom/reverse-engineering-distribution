# Playbook: search (SEO) and answer engines (GEO)

Search is the only distribution surface that keeps working while you sleep, and answer engines are its new front door. Both reward the same thing: a page that answers one question completely.

What to extract:
- **Site size and shape:** total URLs, folders, locales. A sitemap of 3,000 URLs is a machine; a sitemap of 40 is a brochure.
- **Programmatic page families:** what pattern repeats and at what volume — /integrations/{app}, /{a}-vs-{b}, /templates/{use case}, /tools/{calculator}, /glossary/{term}. Describe the template: title pattern, data per page, internal links.
- **Freshness:** content updates in the last 90 days (lastmod), and whether the fresh URLs are new pages or updated old ones.
- **Comparison and alternative pages:** whether they target competitor keywords, and whether they do it honestly (a comparison that names their own weaknesses converts better and is harder to copy badly).
- **The pages that rank:** the top results for the category query, and which of them are theirs, community threads, or directories. A directory or forum outranking the category is an opening.
- **AI-crawler stance:** robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and whether llms.txt exists. Blocking answer engines removes the product from the answer.
- **The definitive answer page:** a page that answers the problem in one place, in plain text, is the page that gets cited.

Copyable output: the page family to build first with its template, the number of pages that makes it a machine (not 3), the freshness cadence, and the answer page to write.
