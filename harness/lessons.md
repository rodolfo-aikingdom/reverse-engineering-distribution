# Lesson library

Format: **cue** → lesson. (Evidence that taught us.) Add a lesson when an eval or a human review shows a recurring miss.

- **Community where under 8% of posts carry a link** → the format does not host product posts. Use it as a mine for the words of the problem; a link post there is removed or ignored. (Measured across subreddits: the pain is discussed in places that do not accept products.)
- **Community rule mentioning "no apps", "no self promotion" or "stealth marketing"** → the posting channel is closed, including the "I'm just sharing a tool I like" version. Name the rule and move the angle to a surface that allows it.
- **Community rule mentioning "AI-generated", "even if it is just for translation"** → an agent-written post violates it even when a human publishes it. Closed, not "careful".
- **Account-age or karma gate in the rules** → it is a date. Schedule the earliest eligible date and use the waiting time for a surface without the gate.
- **No rules read** → unknown, never open. The plan says "ask a moderator" instead of pretending.
- **Posts/week median in a subreddit plus high question share** → an advice surface: replies compound, standalone posts do not.
- **Same hook in their organic top posts and in their longest-running ads** → organic-first testing loop. This is the most copyable play in the bundle: the ads are the organic winners with budget behind them.
- **Ads running 60+ days** → proven winner, not "still running". Lead with these; the run length is the budget proxy.
- **Programmatic page family (hundreds of /x-vs-y, /integrations/, /templates/ pages)** → distribution through search is a machine, not a blog habit. Describe the template and the data per page.
- **Big sitemap, few fresh URLs** → legacy search asset still compounding; new content is not the engine. Do not recommend a publishing calendar that copies it.
- **Founder posting weekly to 10k+** → founder-led distribution; quote what they post about and connect it to the launch cadence.
- **Launch with hundreds of points and no follow-up thread** → the launch was the spike, not the channel. Note what happened in the comments.
- **Store listing carrying the ad's hook** → the listing is the landing page; the ad only buys the click. Report the first line and the screenshots.
- **Affiliate program with published commission plus tracked links** → affiliate is a real channel; the hook library is what affiliates need, not the percentage.
- **Pixel installed but zero ads in the library** → past or paused spend, or ads from another page. Intent, not current spend.
- **Reddit threads ranking on Google for the brand** → Reddit is part of the search surface; note whether the thread is positive or negative, because both spread.
- **A channel with no evidence** → unknown, not absent. Write it in Unknowns with a manual check.
- **An angle that needs the product name to land** → it is an ad wearing a story. Move it to a paid or launch surface, or rewrite it to give something away.
- **A differential that is already a category mechanic** → if the top competitor does the same and has tens of thousands of ratings, it is not a differential. Name it as table stakes.
