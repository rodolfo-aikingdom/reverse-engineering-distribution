# Playbook: launches (Hacker News, Product Hunt, directories)

A launch is a one-day distribution spike that compounds only if the comments, the follow-up thread and the page it points to are ready before it starts.

What to extract:
- **Did they launch, and where:** HN "Show HN" posts with points and comment counts, Product Hunt launches with position and upvotes. One launch or a habit (every release)?
- **The title:** launches that win say what the thing does and include a number or a constraint. Quote the winning title.
- **The first comment:** the founder comment is the offer in miniature: who it is for, what it replaces, why now.
- **What happened in the comments:** objections answered, pricing asked, competitors named. The objections are the copy for the landing page.
- **The follow-up:** a "what we learned from launching" post or a second launch later. Without it, the spike is gone.
- **Directories and aggregators:** which list pages carry the product, and whether they rank for the category query. A listing that ranks is durable distribution.

Copyable output: the launch title pattern, the first-comment structure, the three objections to answer on the landing page, and the post-launch follow-up. One launch, prepared properly, beats three unprepared ones.
