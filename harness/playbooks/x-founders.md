# Playbook: X and founder-led

X distributes through people, not accounts. A company account posts announcements that nobody reads; a founder posting what they are building gets replies from the people who will use it.

What to extract:
- **Who is the account:** the company, a founder, or both. Note the handle and the audience size per person.
- **Cadence and format:** posts/week, threads vs single posts, screenshots vs text, numbers in the first line or not.
- **What they post about:** build logs, revenue numbers, teardowns, opinions, customer replies, memes. Quote the 3 posts with the most engagement.
- **Replies vs broadcasts:** a founder who replies in public to complaints is doing support as distribution. That compounds and cannot be bought.
- **Launch threads:** the thread that announced the product, what the first line said, and what the follow-up was. A launch without a follow-up thread is a spike.
- **What the account sends traffic to:** one link, always the same, or a rotating set of pages.

Copyable output: who should post, at what cadence, the 3 formats that fit this category, and the rule that keeps it honest — no announcement posts, one number or one lesson per post.
