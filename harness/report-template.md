# Report format (Markdown)

Write the report in exactly these sections, in this order. Use the company name in the title.

# How {Company} gets distributed

**One-line verdict:** the engine in one sentence (e.g. "Short-form UGC feeding a free tool, with the winners reused as paid creative and a founder account answering in two niche subreddits").

## TL;DR
3–5 bullets. Each bullet = a play + the proof + why it works. Cite evidence.

## Channel scorecard
A table: Channel | Status | Strength (0–3) | What we saw | Evidence. Use the provided scorecard rows (skip unknown rows with strength 0 unless notable) and sharpen the "what we saw" text.

## The engine
How the surfaces connect into a loop. Name the entry offer, the main CTA, the landing page, and what turns a viewer into a user. Say which surface is the input and which is the multiplier.

## Where we can play
For every audited community and every channel, one line with the verdict: **channel** (product posts are native, our rules allow it), **mine** (language only), **gated** (account must age / karma), **closed** (rules forbid it), **unknown** (rules not read). Cite the rules or the measurement that decided it. Then the surfaces outside communities: search, launches, store, affiliate, paid — with the same verdict logic.

## Distribution plan
A numbered plan, split into **Days 0–7** and **Days 8–30**. Each step: when, surface, the concrete action, why this surface and not another, and the evidence ids. Include the measurement step on day 0 and the settle on day 30. Keep it to what one person with one account can actually run.

## Hooks worth copying
The 5–8 hooks that are winning for them, quoted exactly, each with where it ran and what it did. Group by pattern (problem-first, POV, teardown, number, before/after) and say which pattern repeats across surfaces.

## Funnel & offer
Entry offer, pricing model, trial/free plan, CTA, what page the traffic lands on, and the proof they show (ratings, logos, numbers). Say what a person sees in the first 10 seconds.

## What we could not see
Bullet list: what we could not see, why, and the manual-check link or the credential that would close it.

## Unknowns & how to check
Bullet list of unknown channels with the manual-check link from the bundle.
