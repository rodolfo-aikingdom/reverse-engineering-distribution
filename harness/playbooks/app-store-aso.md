# Playbook: app stores (ASO)

The store listing is a landing page that sits inside a search engine with its own ranking rules, and it is the destination of every ad and bio link.

What to extract:
- **Title and subtitle:** the keyword the title carries, and the promise the subtitle makes. Quote both.
- **The first three screenshots:** what they show (feature, outcome, social proof, price). The first screenshot is the hook.
- **Ratings and count:** rating average and number of ratings as a proxy for install volume and age. Thousands of ratings means the ranking is already earned.
- **Release age and update cadence:** a two-year-old app updated monthly is compounding; a six-month-old app with no updates is stuck.
- **The description's first paragraph:** what problem it claims, in whose words.
- **Custom product pages and campaign tokens:** if ads link to the store with ppid/ct parameters, the store page is the ad's landing page and each campaign has its own variant.
- **Reviews:** the one-star and three-star reviews name the thing that stops the install, which is usually the same thing the ads promise.

Copyable output: the title/subtitle pattern, the first-screenshot rule, the review objections to fix before spending on installs, and whether to build a custom product page per campaign.
