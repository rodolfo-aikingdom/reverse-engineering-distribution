# Playbook: offer and funnel

Distribution fails at the offer more often than at the channel. A hook that gets the click and a page that does not convert wastes the reach twice.

What to extract:
- **The entry offer:** free plan, free trial with a length, free tool, lead magnet, quiz, community. It decides which traffic is worth buying.
- **The CTA:** the exact words on the primary button, repeated across pages. Quote it.
- **Pricing model:** flat, per seat, usage/credits, lifetime deal, sales-assisted tier. The model tells you who they sell to and how the channel must be shaped.
- **The proof above the fold:** ratings with counts, logos, user numbers, "as seen in". Quote the number.
- **The landing page structure:** headline (problem or outcome), subhead (mechanism), proof, one CTA, objection handling below.
- **The first 10 seconds:** what a person arriving from a TikTok or a Reddit thread sees. If the page does not name the problem in the hook's words, the click is lost.
- **Lifecycle:** welcome email, trial expiry, winback. Client-side tools are visible; server-side ones are unknown, and saying so is part of the report.

Copyable output: the entry offer to match or beat, the CTA text, the proof to collect before launching, and the landing page outline in the order the visitor reads it.
