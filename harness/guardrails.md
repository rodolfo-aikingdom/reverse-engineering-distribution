# Evidence rules (non-negotiable)

- Every factual sentence cites evidence ids in square brackets, e.g. "[E4]" or "[E4, E9]". Cite the specific item, not a whole source.
- Distinguish four kinds of statements and label inferences:
  - **Observation**: seen directly (a post, a pixel, a follower count, a sitemap URL). Cite it.
  - **Derivation**: computed from observations (posts/week, % of posts with links). Cite it.
  - **Inference**: your interpretation. Prefix it with "Likely" or "Probably" and cite the evidence it rests on.
  - **Unknown**: not checked or not visible. Never treat unknown as "not doing it". Put it in the Unknowns section with the manual-check link from the bundle.
- Never invent numbers, ad copy, hooks, founders, budgets, revenue, results or community rules. Spend is not public: talk about spend proxies (number of active ads, ad longevity, launch rate).
- A pixel proves intent or past use, not current spend. Say "pixel installed", not "running ads", unless the ad library confirms it.
- Ad library counts come from samples. Say "in the sample".
- Quote hooks, post titles and ad copy exactly as given in the evidence, trimmed with "…" if needed.

# Distribution rules (also non-negotiable)

- **A community we did not read the rules of is unknown, not open.** If no rules were read, say so and put "ask a moderator before posting" in the plan. Never infer tolerance for promotion from the content of the posts.
- **A rule that forbids apps, self-promotion, AI-written text or our identity closes the channel.** Do not propose a workaround, a "subtle" version, or posting it from a friend's account. Say the channel is closed and move the angle to one that is open.
- **An account-age or karma gate is a date, not an obstacle to route around.** Report the gate and schedule the earliest date the account qualifies.
- **A community where almost no post carries a link is a language mine.** Use it to harvest the words of the problem; do not plan a product post there.
- **Do not propose paying for reach before there is a winning organic artifact.** Paid amplifies a hook that already worked; it does not create one.
- **Do not recommend an angle that only works if the product is named.** That is an ad. The angles that survive in a community add something without the link.
- **Do not count reach, impressions or followers as a result.** The number that decides is the one in the funnel (signups, activations, replies that turn into conversations), and if it was not measured, say it was not measured.
- **Copy the strategy, never the assets.** Study their hooks, cadence, page templates and offers; do not reproduce their creatives, footage, copy or brand. Same rule as clean-room engineering: study, then ship your own.
